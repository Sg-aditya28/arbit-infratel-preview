Pioneering Infrastructure & Commercial Real Estate Since 2010

Incorporated under RoC Kanpur in Gorakhpur, Arbit Infratel Private Limited has expanded from utility telecommunications engineering into one of Eastern Uttar Pradesh's leading commercial real estate developers and turnkey civil EPC contractors.
